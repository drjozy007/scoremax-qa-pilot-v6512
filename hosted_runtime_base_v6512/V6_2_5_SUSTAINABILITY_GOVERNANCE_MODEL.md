# Sustainability Governance Model

## Claim stages

- `CURRENT_PRACTICE`: implemented and supportable by present evidence.
- `IN_PROGRESS`: started but not complete.
- `FUTURE_COMMITMENT`: a dated ambition, never presented as achieved.

## Required fields

Each commitment carries category, description, baseline, target, target date, status, owner, evidence summary and public status.

## Publication controls

- Manual Admin publishing only.
- Growth Engine drafts enter `DRAFT_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.
- Progress reports may acknowledge missed targets and revised actions.
- Environmental impact is not claimed until a measurable baseline exists.
