# V6.2.8 Commercial Entitlement Contract

## Two independent dimensions

1. **Coverage package** — which subjects in the selected programme are included.
2. **Access tier** — Free Access, Level 1 Access, Level 2 Access or Full Access.

These must never be conflated with mastery labels.

## Subject states

- **Included:** server authorises the subject and the selected feature tier.
- **Locked — Upgrade:** the subject exists but is outside the active coverage package.
- **Coming Soon:** ScoreMax has declared the subject/programme but no releasable governed inventory exists.

## Enforcement

Server-side checks apply to subject entry, chapters, test setup/start, Test Me, mastery assessment and Study Plan activity launch. UI hiding alone is not treated as access control.

## Persistence

Upgrading or replacing an entitlement does not delete previous attempts, results, mastery or Study Plan evidence. Entitlement history records the previous and new package state.

## Payment boundary

V6.2.8 provides package/pricing administration, manual/institutional assignment and a provider-neutral pending checkout request. It does not claim that live card processing, refunds, recurring billing or tax handling are operational.
