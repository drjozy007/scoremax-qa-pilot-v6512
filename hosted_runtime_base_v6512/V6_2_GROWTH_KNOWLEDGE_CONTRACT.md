# Growth Engine → ScoreMax Knowledge Hub Draft Contract

The Growth Engine may submit a draft containing title, summary, body, framework/version, subject/chapter, proposed sources and an external draft ID.

Every intake is stored as:

```text
DRAFT → human review → source/rights verification → publication decision
```

The Growth Engine cannot activate the Knowledge Hub, approve a source, or publish an article. Commercial books/PDFs must not be hosted without permission. Official or legally public documents may be linked with a recorded rights status.
