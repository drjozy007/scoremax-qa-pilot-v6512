- Rubric-only constructed responses without a governed human-mark delivery route are explicitly rejected with `UNSUPPORTED_RUBRIC_ONLY_DELIVERY`; a future human-mark path would require separate governance and acceptance.
- Inherited real-browser/accessibility, SMTP and founder live-acceptance gates remain pending where applicable.
- Integrated adversarial qualification across the three deployed systems remains pending.
