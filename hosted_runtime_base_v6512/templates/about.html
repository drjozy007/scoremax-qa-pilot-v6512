ABOUT US

We want exams to become a pathway to improvement, not just a final score.

ScoreMax is being built to help students understand where they are losing marks, what they should improve next, and whether their work is actually making a difference.

OUR VISION

Help every student see a clear path towards their academic goal.

OUR MISSION

Turn testing into useful feedback, targeted improvement and measurable progress.

What makes ScoreMax different?

We are not trying to become another lecture-heavy online academy. ScoreMax starts with your test results: test yourself, find the exact problem, get short help where needed, practise it, and test again.
