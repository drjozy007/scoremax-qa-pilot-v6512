## Critical invariants covered

- Assisted correctness cannot close mastery.
- Variants/dependents cannot manufacture independent mastery routes.
- Near-copy-only evidence cannot satisfy a required unseen-transfer gate.
- A mandatory Claim Family gate can block/reopen otherwise strong mastery.
- A later qualifying correction can recover a misconception gate.
- SOURCE_ONLY evidence cannot leak into independent node/family closure.
- QA evidence cannot affect LIVE states.
- Time passing can make evidence maintenance-due without declaring forgetting.
- An isolated interleaved/authentic-exam failure does not automatically erase verified subject knowledge.
- Repeated contradictory blocked evidence can reopen the affected entity.
- A prerequisite gap routes recovery to the prerequisite rather than automatically blaming the parent concept.
- MDCAT/NEET/JEE scoring is resolved by the active rule set.
- Correct-but-slow can preserve subject mastery while exam fluency remains developing.
- High-confidence wrong receives elevated risk handling.
- EV/attempt analytics remain descriptive before calibration.
- SYLLABUS_NOVEL cannot become 'low yield' solely because historical papers do not exist.
- Repair Cost does not invent learner-specific precision at cold start.
- Full-exam pacing/entry/fatigue opportunity has zero automatic content-seed attribution.
- Fixed evidence + fixed ruleset replays to the same mastery output/input checksum.
