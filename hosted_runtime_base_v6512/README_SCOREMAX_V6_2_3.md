# ScoreMax V6.2.3

## Student Command Centre & Adaptive UX

This release converts the student experience from a long portal-style series of cards into a compact application organised around the student's next decision.

### Student Command Centre

The dashboard now prioritises:

1. the best next action;
2. the student's current evidence position;
3. one ScoreMax Coach recommendation;
4. four core shortcuts: Practice, My Plan, Exams and Progress.

Secondary information is placed inside Overview, Subjects, Progress and Activity tabs. Only one panel is visible at a time.

### Adaptive Study Plan workload

Core, Stretch and Peak remain preparation pathways. They now communicate coverage pace, testing rhythm and recovery depth rather than promising a universal number of minutes per day.

ScoreMax calculates a weekly recommendation range from pathway, exam proximity, syllabus coverage and target. The student's realistic weekly availability is stored separately and reported as Realistic, Needs prioritisation or Comfortable capacity.

### Matric pathway intelligence

Matric/SSC students see a broader current-subject catalogue independently from live question inventory. Subjects can be Live or Coming Soon.

Pathway Explorer supports:

- FSc/HSSC Pre-Medical;
- FSc/HSSC Pre-Engineering;
- ICS/Computer Science;
- ICom/Commerce;
- FA/Humanities;
- Still deciding.

Future assessments such as MDCAT and ECAT are displayed as available or exploratory without being misrepresented as current Matric tests.

### Testing and support

Design My Own Test supports up to 60 questions and timing choices of untimed, 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes. Authentic mock timing remains governed by the assessment blueprint.

Report an Issue no longer asks students for database identifiers. Question, attempt, page, blueprint and policy context are captured automatically where available.

### Progressive page design

Study Plan, Exam Centre and Progress now use tabs and expandable details. FAQs are searchable and updated for the current platform. Knowledge Hub and verified official social channels are discoverable without dominating the learning navigation.

### ScoreMax Coach

The Coach selects one evidence-aware action using priorities such as:

- continue an in-progress assessment;
- complete the next Study Plan action;
- choose a post-Matric pathway;
- take a first diagnostic;
- complete due recall;
- recover a verified weakness;
- set a goal.

Suggestions are collapsed outside the dashboard and support Open, Later and Dismiss controls.

### Integrity correction

The provisional performance ladder now uses only:

`Foundation → Exam Ready → Advanced → Distinction → Expert → Elite`

Study Plan pathway labels Stretch and Peak cannot be emitted as mastery/performance levels.
