# V6.2.3 Changelog

## Added

- compact Student Command Centre;
- tabbed dashboard workspace;
- ScoreMax Coach events, snooze and dismissal;
- Matric academic catalogue independent from question inventory;
- post-Matric Pathway Explorer;
- Live versus Coming Soon subject availability;
- official social-channel registry and public Connect page;
- automatic issue-report context;
- searchable role-relevant FAQs;
- 60-question and 60-minute custom-test options;
- adaptive weekly Study Plan workload ranges;
- migration utility and 33 dedicated UX regression checks.

## Changed

- student navigation is now Dashboard, Learn, My Plan, Exams, Progress and More;
- Find a Teacher and Academic Messages moved under supporting navigation;
- Study Plan, Exam Centre and Progress use progressive tabs;
- current-study pages no longer append every unrelated live subject from other markets;
- Knowledge Hub and official channel links are visible from the shell;
- issue reports validate linked attempts and capture hidden identifiers automatically.

## Fixed

- Advanced and Elite can no longer be incorrectly represented as Stretch or Peak;
- unknown syllabus coverage no longer inflates every workload recommendation as though coverage were 0%;
- mastery-level analytics sort in the official six-level order.

## Preserved

- V6.2.1 session-integrity fix;
- V6.2 content-intake atomicity and rollback protections;
- V6.1 teacher/messaging safeguarding;
- V6 written-response evidence integrity;
- V5.5 blueprint, calibration and historical pinning.
