# ScoreMax V6.2.7 Academic Reviewer Workspace Contract

## Purpose

Provide traceable question-by-question academic review without exposing ScoreMax architecture, student data, live mastery or the wider platform.

## Reviewer-visible fields only

- chapter and topic;
- one question at a time;
- response options or answer area;
- configured correct answer;
- explanation;
- proposed mastery level;
- mastery-level suitability;
- review decision;
- comments where required.

## Confidentiality boundary

Reviewers receive named accounts, one-time time-limited invitations, reviewer-specific watermarks and assigned-batch-only access. They cannot use normal ScoreMax navigation, browse the live bank, export a batch, see student data or publish questions.

## Assignment rules

- 1–100 questions per assignment;
- autosave after each submitted decision;
- exact resume at the next unfinished item;
- completed items may be revisited within the assigned batch;
- active time is recorded only through bounded visible-and-active browser ticks;
- time is a quality-risk signal, never automatic proof of review quality.

## Decisions

- Accept unchanged;
- Correction required;
- Mastery level unsuitable;
- Reject;
- Unsure.

Comments are mandatory for every decision except Accept unchanged.

## Governance

Anything not accepted 100% unchanged on first review is routed to an independent second reviewer. Matching decisions are recorded as agreement. Disagreement creates an adjudication requirement. Reviewers cannot publish or promote questions.
