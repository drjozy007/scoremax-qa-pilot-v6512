# V6.1 Trust, Safety and Professional Boundaries

## Relationship rules

- A teacher cannot open a direct student conversation.
- A student must choose a published service and send a structured enquiry.
- The teacher must accept the enquiry.
- A group requires a published teacher listing, separate group moderation, student request and teacher approval.
- Student-to-student private messaging is not implemented.

## Identity and claims

- Identity, qualification and experience checks are separate statuses.
- Verification badges state what was checked.
- Verification does not imply teaching quality or academic endorsement.
- Public profiles and listings are moderated.

## Minors

- DOB is required for messaging eligibility.
- Under-18 users require active linked-parent consent.
- Consent is versioned and separately stored.
- Consent is re-checked at enquiry acceptance, group approval and message send.
- Revocation suspends access without destroying evidence.

## Content controls

Automatic pilot holds cover:

- phone numbers;
- private email addresses;
- WhatsApp references;
- payment-transfer requests;
- requests for secrecy/off-platform concealment;
- simple abusive-language patterns;
- unapproved external links;
- student-sent meeting links;
- teacher meeting links outside approved domains.

This pre-filter reduces obvious risk but does not replace trained moderation or a validated production classifier.

## Reporting and evidence

- users can Report and Block;
- automatic holds generate reports;
- held messages remain invisible to recipients;
- authorised Admin can remove messages or suspend users;
- evidence is preserved for audit;
- moderator access must be operationally restricted and logged.

## Ratings

- ratings require a relationship created through ScoreMax;
- teacher and student both confirm the interaction;
- one review per verified engagement;
- reviews require moderation;
- aggregate ratings use only published verified reviews.
