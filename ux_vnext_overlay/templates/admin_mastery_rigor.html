ADMIN · ACADEMIC GOVERNANCE

Mastery & Rigor

Control how demanding future ScoreMax assessments are and how much evidence a learner must accumulate before a mastery level is verified. Historical attempts and previously earned evidence are never rewritten.

Operating principle

Mastery % remains understandable learner performance. These controls govern the strength, breadth, novelty, complexity and recency of evidence required before ScoreMax trusts that performance enough to award a mastery level.

GLOBAL CALIBRATION

Assessment Rigor & Mastery Standard

{% if active_global %}Active global policy · v{{active_global.policy_version}}{% endif %}

50/100 is the neutral baseline. Raising Mastery Standard tightens future mastery evidence requirements. Raising Assessment Rigor shifts future forms toward harder and more novel evidence.

A new policy is saved as DRAFT first. It must then be activated explicitly. If a stricter policy is activated, existing verified mastery is not deleted; affected records become Verification Due where the existing governed logic requires reconfirmation.
